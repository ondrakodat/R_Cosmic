## Observed layout

- 26 pages, one portrait A4 section, different first-page header/footer.
- Margins: top 1.181 in; left, right, and bottom about 0.985 in; header/footer about 0.492 in.
- Cover centered: university at top; a large vertical gap; italic assignment label and bold paper title; author/study details; date near page bottom.
- Body uses Times New Roman 12 pt. Heading 1 uses Arial 14 pt bold with 24 pt before; Heading 2 uses 13 pt bold with 10 pt before. Captions are about 10 pt and bold.
- Chapter headings use a numbered hierarchy. Tables have captions and restrained table formatting; figures are centered and captions directly adjacent. A page number appears in the regular footer, while the cover has no footer number.
